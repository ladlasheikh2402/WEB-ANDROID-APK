This Privacy Policy explains how Ladla Sheikh ("we", "us", "our", operated by Sheikh Ali Ahmad, Okara, Pakistan, at ladla-sheikh.vercel.app) collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you visit the site, create an account or sign in, browse or download tools, install the site as an app (PWA), or use our contact form. Some sections of the site (About, Tools, Methods, Contact) require you to be signed in — the Home section is always public. By using this website you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

1. Information we collect

Account information

Some sections of the site (About, Tools, Methods, Contact) require an account — the Home section is always open to everyone. When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Email/password sign-up: your name, a username, your email address, and a password. Your password is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication (Google's authentication service) — it's hashed and stored by Firebase, and we never see or store your actual password ourselves (see How we protect your data).
  • Social sign-in (Google / GitHub): the basic profile info that provider shares with us — typically your name and email address. We never see or store your social account password.

Your account's name, username, email and sign-in provider are stored in our Firebase Realtime Database. A small flag is also kept in your browser's local storage so the site remembers you're signed in between visits.

Information you give us directly

  • Contact form submissions — your name, email, phone number and message.
  • Anything you choose to send us via WhatsApp, Telegram, or email when you reach out through the links on the site.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data — browser type, general location (from IP address), pages visited, and how you interact with the site (this may come from our hosting provider's standard logs and, if enabled, analytics tools).
  • If you install the site as an app (PWA — "Add to Home Screen"), basic install/usage state so the install prompt behaves correctly. This is stored on your device.
  • If you play the background audio on the site, your last volume/play preference, stored locally on your device only — we don't receive this.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and let you sign in securely.
  • To respond to messages sent through the contact form or via WhatsApp/Telegram/email.
  • To keep the site secure — detecting abuse, spam, and automated sign-up/form submissions.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it and the tools we share.
  • To meet legal obligations where applicable.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Signing in with Google / GitHub

When you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with GitHub", you're shown that provider's own sign-in screen to authenticate, via Firebase Authentication. We only receive the profile fields the provider allows — usually your name and email address — governed by that provider's own consent screen. Your use of Google or GitHub is also subject to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

Facebook, phone-number (SMS) and Apple sign-in are not currently offered on this site — if any of these are added later, this policy will be updated to cover them.

4. Tools, downloads and third-party apps

The tools, mods and APKs listed on this site (WhatsApp/Telegram mods, premium "unlocked" versions of third-party apps, editing and design tools, and similar files) are hosted and downloaded directly from GitHub Releases, not from our own servers. When you tap "Download":

  • You leave this site and download the file directly from GitHub. GitHub's own privacy policy and terms apply to that download.
  • We don't track who downloads which file, and we don't receive any personal data from that action.
  • Installing and using any downloaded APK is at your own discretion and risk — please review app permissions before installing, especially for modified versions of third-party apps.

A few listed tools are explicitly labelled for educational/demo purposes only (for example, fake-chat or prank-style tools). We don't collect any data from your use of these tools, and you're responsible for using them lawfully and only with people who consent.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use a small amount of local storage and cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in between visits (Firebase Authentication stores your session on your device).
  • Remember install-prompt state and your preferences (e.g. audio volume) on your device.
  • Understand aggregate site usage, if analytics tools are enabled.

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time from your browser settings. Doing so may sign you out or reset saved preferences (like the install prompt or audio volume).

6. How we share information

We share information only in these situations:

  • Service providers who help us run the site — our hosting provider (Vercel) and Firebase (Google) for authentication and our database. They only receive what's needed to perform their function, and are bound to protect it.
  • GitHub — tool/APK downloads are served directly from GitHub Releases; GitHub receives whatever standard request data your browser sends when downloading a file (see Tools, downloads and third-party apps).
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Ladla Sheikh, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — if the site or business is ever transferred, your data may transfer as part of that, under the same protections described here.

7. How we protect your data

We take the following measures to keep your data safe:

  • Passwords are never stored or seen by us in plain text. Email/password sign-in is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication, which hashes and stores your password using industry-standard methods — we don't have access to it.
  • All traffic is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Database access rules restrict who can write account data — a signed-in user can only create or edit their own profile, and usernames can't be taken over once claimed.
  • Access is limited to Ladla Sheikh (Sheikh Ali Ahmad) only — we don't have a team with broader access to your account or messages.
  • Rate limiting and abuse detection on sign-up/sign-in and the contact form to reduce brute-force, credential-stuffing and spam attempts.
  • Regular dependency and security updates to close known vulnerabilities on the site itself.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so while we work hard to protect your information, we can't guarantee absolute security. Please also do your part: use a strong, unique password, and never share your login details.

8. Data retention

We keep your account information (name, username, email, sign-in provider) for as long as your account exists. This site doesn't currently have a self-service "delete account" button — if you'd like your account and data removed, see how to delete your data and we'll remove or anonymize it within a reasonable period, except where we're legally required to retain it. Contact form messages are kept only for as long as needed to respond to you and for a reasonable period afterward for reference, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent for further communication with us at any time.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below.

If you click through and interact with these, their own privacy policies apply, and we aren't responsible for how they handle your data. If you message us on WhatsApp or Telegram, that conversation is also governed by WhatsApp's/Telegram's own privacy policy in addition to this one.
